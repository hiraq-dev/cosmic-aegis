## Plain text (for a teleprompter or text-to-speech)
Mars is twenty minutes away by radio. A medical emergency can't wait forty.

AEGIS is an offline medical assistant for deep space. Cameras track exercise form and warn of fatigue, with no wearables.

A second, separate pipeline reads a visible symptom with a vision-language model, then retrieves a cited treatment plan from offline space-medicine protocols.

Each part runs in its own container. If one fails, the other keeps working.

C.O.S.M.I.C. AEGIS. Autonomous. Offline. Resilient.

## Recording notes
- Record the screen first and trim it to exactly 30 s, then record the voice-over against it.
- Pre-load the sample image and pre-type the stop command so nothing waits during the take.
- Say the name as "cosmic aegis" (aegis = "EE-jis"); the end card shows the full spelling.
- No NASA logos or mission patches on any card.
- If you use a synthetic (text-to-speech) voice, say so in the video description and in the form's SI-use field; audio generated by AI must be acknowledged there.
- Don't claim anything the demo doesn't show. If the VLM is the mock backend, keep the "SIMULATED" caption and consider changing "reads a visible symptom with a vision-language model" to "demonstrates reading a visible symptom".
